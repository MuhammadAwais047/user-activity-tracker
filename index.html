        How trace works

        The whole pipeline, in four steps

        01

        Instrument

        One track() call records the type, label, screen, session, and timestamp of an interaction.

        02

        Persist

        Events and sessions are written to localStorage on every capture. Refresh and the history is still there.

        03

        Aggregate

        Ranges, deltas, daily series, hourly buckets, and type mixes are computed on the fly — no analytics SDK.

        04

        Read

        Charts, a rhythm heatmap, session replay, filters, and JSON export turn raw rows into a story.
